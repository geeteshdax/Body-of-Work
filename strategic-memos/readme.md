# Strategic Memos

- Short-form written analysis on specific business questions.
- Each memo takes a view and defends it.

---

## What A Memo Is

Seven sections. One question that matters.
- A judgement on it. 
- A confidence level assigned to that judgement.
= What the analysis found. 
- What argues against it.
- What to watch that would change the view.
- Why any of it matters.

No summaries. No balanced takes. A specific view,  held with honesty about where it could be wrong.
