# Zepto: Can It Actually Reach Breakeven?

The question: Zepto lost over ₹5,000 crore on an adjusted EBITDA basis in FY26. Blinkit, at similar scale, just crossed into profit. Is Zepto's path to adjusted EBITDA breakeven credible — and what does Blinkit's arrival prove about how far Zepto still has to go?

## The Judgment

- Zepto's losses are a deliberate expansion choice, not a broken model — the path to profitability exists and is being deferred, not chased.
- The mechanism that turns stores profitable is proven next door at Blinkit; Zepto simply hasn't let its network age into it yet.
- Breakeven is therefore a decision about pace, not a question of capability.

Zepto can reach adjusted EBITDA breakeven, but not at its current expansion pace — the same store maturation that made Blinkit profitable requires letting the store base age, which fights Zepto's growth-first posture.

## Confidence

- High: The improvement drivers are real and two of three compound structurally rather than run out.
- Medium: Whether Zepto can afford to wait for its stores to mature at its current spending pace.

## What we found

- The real drain is getting orders to doors and buying customers — not the headline cost of stocking shelves.
- In-app advertising became a genuine margin engine, but it's a lever already largely pulled, not fresh runway.
- Delivery got cheaper by bundling neighbourhood orders under one rider, trading strict speed for efficiency.
- Order volume nearly doubled, so total losses grew even as each order lost less — expansion masking progress.
- The same maturation curve that made Blinkit profitable is visibly available to Zepto but deliberately unused.
- Management misread its own distance to breakeven — the gap was several times wider than it guided.

## What's against this

- Blinkit funds its expansion from its own profits; Zepto funds the same fight by burning outside money.
- The profitable player can outspend the loss-making one indefinitely, and that advantage compounds every quarter.
- Zepto's mature-store health is assumed from Blinkit's pattern, not proven from Zepto's own network.
- Aggressive expansion keeps the blended picture depressed by design, delaying any visible turn.
- The 10-minute promise is quietly being traded away for cost efficiency, at some cost to the brand.
- Slowing down to harvest maturation would concede ground to a rival growing faster and already ahead.
