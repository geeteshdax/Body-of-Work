# Industry Situation Map

## What is this industry, and what's the central tension right now?

- Quick commerce is a delivery format layered on grocery and daily-use retail, not a standalone product category.
- The central tension is not who has the best app. It's who can afford to lose money the longest.
- Six players compete today, split cleanly by one structural fact: whether a profitable sibling business funds their losses.
- Blinkit draws on Eternal's food-delivery cash flow. Instamart draws on Swiggy's.
- Zepto has no such sibling. It depends entirely on external capital to keep competing.
- That single difference, not execution, is the thread running through every section that follows.
- The market has already moved from three serious players to six in under two years.
- Amazon Now and Flipkart Minutes are not experiments anymore. Both are treating this as core strategy.
- The industry is still expanding physically, not consolidating. Store counts, not store closures, define this phase.
- Whether that expansion is sustainable, or who survives it, is what the rest of this report answers.
