# Quick Commerce in India — Industry Research

An independent structural investigation into India's quick-commerce sector: unit economics, moat durability, and where the industry is actually headed.

## The Core Finding

No quick-commerce business in India has a durable moat at the operating level. Dark-store density, private label, and app stickiness are all thin and replicable within a year or two — Amazon and Flipkart proved this in real time by replicating years of incumbent buildout in about 24 months.

The one advantage that has held up under stress isn't inside any quick-commerce business at all. It's whether that business sits next to a profitable sibling company that can fund it through a downturn. Blinkit draws on Eternal's food-delivery cash flow. Instamart draws on Swiggy's. Zepto, as a standalone pure-play, does not — and public markets are already pricing that gap directly into its IPO valuation.

## Method
Evidence is prioritized in order: primary filings and disclosures, official data, industry research, journalism, and commentary last. Every conflicting figure encountered is flagged, not silently resolved.
