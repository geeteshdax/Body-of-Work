# The Profit Pool

## Where is money actually made, and is that where everyone's looking?

- Four revenue streams exist: product margin, delivery fees, advertising, and private label.
- Product margin runs thin on staples, far richer on categories like beauty and electronics.
- Take rate roughly doubled in three years, from 7–9% to about 18%.
- Platform-fee revenue is projected to grow faster than order volume itself through FY28.
- That specific fact is the most structural finding in this section. It doesn't depend on any one company's choices.
- Advertising is the fastest-growing, highest-margin stream. It is also the least disclosed.
- Combined industry ad revenue is estimated near ₹4,900 crore for 2026. No major platform states this figure directly.
- Zepto's own DRHP puts its ad revenue at ₹1,636 crore for FY26. That is the one filed number available.
- This matters because ad revenue sits on top of thin or negative delivery economics.
- Whether "improving unit economics" means delivery got better, or ad revenue grew, cannot currently be separated.
- Private label carries 7–8% margins on staples, well above thin third-party commissions.
- Industry estimates suggest just 8–10% of sales shifting to private label would meaningfully move profitability.
- All three major players are building private label in the same categories, at the same time.
- That parallel pursuit makes private label a margin lever for the industry, not a differentiator for any one player yet.
