# Forces & Constraints

## What shapes this industry from outside — regulation, capital, technology, labor?

- Four external forces bear on this industry, and none of them are settled background.
- Foreign capital is barred from inventory-owned domestic e-commerce. This is why platforms shift toward owning inventory carefully.
- That inventory-ownership shift also inflates headline revenue figures, independent of actual demand growth.
- A July 2026 policy relaxation opened FDI for export-only inventory models. Domestic quick commerce remains excluded.
- Food safety enforcement has already materialized, not just been threatened. Zepto's parent had a Mumbai license suspended.
- Blinkit separately had a Pune dark store closed over an alleged licensing violation.
- Both incidents cite fungal contamination or hygiene lapses tied to perishable-goods handling.
- This connects directly to Blinkit's own disclosed inventory loss, roughly 1.8% of order value, largely from perishables.
- One operational weakness is showing up as both a cost line and a compliance risk simultaneously.
- FSSAI now requires 30% of a product's shelf life, or 45 days, remaining at delivery time.
- That rule constrains how aggressively platforms can sell near-expiry stock to protect margin.
