## The Insight

The entire investment case hinges on one question: **Which scenario plays out?**

Market is pricing bull case (₹401 at 52.8x P/E implies 18–20% growth). Management guides base case (10–13% growth). Our analysis weights all three (Conservative 25%, Base 50%, Bull 25%), yielding probability-weighted fair value of ~₹293, or approximately **19% downside from ₹401.**

The disconnect between market assumption and management reality is the entire thesis.

---

## Why It Matters

Valuation is not about precision. It's about whether the price being paid reflects realistic probability of outcomes.

At ₹401, you're betting that VBL will grow 18–20% sustainably. But management says 10–13%. Either management is wrong (conservative), or the market is overpricing (more likely).

Disciplined investors buy when market assumes pessimism (upside), not when it assumes optimism (downside).
