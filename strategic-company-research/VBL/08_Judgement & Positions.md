# TOPIC 9: JUDGMENT & POSITIONS

**Read Time:** 7 minutes  
**Key Takeaway:** We bought VBL at fair price, took profits on strength. Fair value thesis still valid; waiting for re-entry.  
**Who Should Read This:** Investors seeing real conviction backed by real capital, understanding how to position for uncertainty

---

## Consensus View

**Wall Street consensus on VBL:** BUY / HOLD. Target price ₹561 (40% upside from ₹401).

The consensus argument: "VBL is India's largest beverage bottler with an exceptional distribution moat. Yes, margins are under pressure from Campa, but it's temporary pricing disruption. Once Sting scales and Africa proves profitable, earnings accelerate to 18–20% CAGR. Stock is down from ₹568 but still has 40% upside to analyst targets."

Most analysts see weakness as buying opportunity. Most see the thesis as intact. Most recommend accumulation on dips.

---

## What We Weighed Differently

**Three core disagreements with consensus:**

**1. Fair value timing, not direction.**

Consensus is right that VBL is a quality business. But right business ≠ right price. At ₹401, fair value is NOT yet captured. Fair value band is ₹240–300. That's 40–50% downside, not 40% upside.

I didn't bet against VBL. I bet against the **premium that ₹401 represents.** The stock needed to re-rate down, not up. My exit at ₹463 (when I should have waited for ₹240–300) proved this hypothesis wrong temporarily. But the thesis—that fair value is ₹240–300—hasn't changed.

**2. Africa execution risk is higher than consensus prices.**

Consensus assumes Africa will hit 18–22% ROCE by FY27. I weighted it at 65% probability, not 90%. If Africa delivers 12–15% ROCE instead, capex is value-destructive and fair value drops to ₹180–220. That's the tail risk consensus isn't pricing in.

**3. Campa disruption is structural, not cyclical.**

Consensus sees Campa as a 2–3 year pricing headwind that normalizes. I see it as proof that VBL's pricing power is permanently diminished. The duopoly is broken. Fragmentation is structural. Margins may never recover to 18–20%. They might stabilize at 16–17% (still good, but not great). That's a ₹50–100 haircut to fair value vs. consensus.

---

## Key Assumptions

Our thesis rests on three bets:

**1. Fair value ₹240–300 is correct** (probability: 70%)
- Depends on: Base case 10–13% growth, 13–14% margins, 24–28x P/E multiple
- If wrong because: Growth accelerates to 15%+ or margins recover to 16%+ (bull case becomes real)

**2. Stock will correct to fair value within 18–24 months** (probability: 60%)
- Depends on: Market repricing bull case to base case
- If wrong because: Market stays euphoric and keeps VBL at 45–50x P/E (tail risk)

**3. Africa execution risk is material** (probability: 65% we're right to be cautious)
- Depends on: Africa ROCE turns out 12–18%, not 18–22%
- If wrong because: Africa ROCE exceeds 20% and drives unexpected upside

---

## Confidence Level

**HIGH on the business quality (80%)** — VBL is genuinely an excellent operator. Management executes, moat is real, capital returns are strong.

**MEDIUM on the valuation (60%)** — Fair value ₹240–300 is our best estimate, but ranges ₹200–350 if assumptions shift. Valuation is more art than science.

**MEDIUM on the timing (55%)** — We don't know if re-rating happens in 12 months or 36 months. Recovery is real, but patience has a cost.

**MEDIUM-LOW on Africa execution (50%)** — Unproven investment. Could deliver 20%+ ROCE or disappoint at 12%. Too early to tell.

**Overall conviction: MEDIUM (60%).** We believe the thesis, but execution + timing are uncertain. That's why we exited at ₹463.

---

## What Would Change Our Mind

**Thesis-validating news (would add back):**

1. **FY26 results show Africa EBITDA margin >12%** → Capex thesis on track. Would re-rate fair value to ₹300–350. Add aggressively.

2. **Q2 FY27 shows FCF inflecting positive** → Capex cycle peak confirmed. Cash generation resuming. Fair value improves to ₹320–380. Add.

3. **Sting reaches 5–6% of revenue by Q3 FY27** → Premium mix thesis working. Margins recovering. Valuation multiple deserves expansion. Add.

4. **Analyst consensus downgrades to ₹400 target** → Market repricing to base case. Gap to fair value narrows. Setup becomes better. Add.

**Thesis-breaking news (would exit or stay out):**

1. **FY26 net margins fall below 12%** → Structural compression, not seasonal dip. Fair value drops to ₹150–200. Stay out until clarity.

2. **Africa loss announcement / write-down** → Capex thesis broken. Management credibility erodes. Fair value to ₹220–260. Wait for panic selling.

3. **Campa gains to 25%+ share** → Duopoly permanently broken; pricing power gone. CSD margins stay at 12–13% forever. Fair value to ₹180–220. Exit or avoid.

4. **Revenue growth turns negative** → Growth story broken. Back to ₹150–200. Avoid.

5. **Stock falls to ₹250–300 and holds** → Fair value zone reached. Risk/reward flips to favorable. This would be buy signal.

## Action Taken

**Entry:** ₹10,000 invested at ₹401 on March 19, 2026

**Position:** 24–25 shares at ₹401/share

**Rationale then:** Medium conviction on base case thesis. Fair value ₹240–300 meant downside risk, but upside from multiple re-rating (if bull case emerged) was meaningful. Wanted "learning position" + real money on the line while monitoring quarterly.

**Exit:** Sold entire position at ₹463 in April 2026

**Exit rationale:** Stock rallied 15%+ in 3 weeks. This was opportunity cost capture, not thesis validation. If fair value is ₹240–300, holding at ₹463 meant:
- No margin of safety (actually negative safety, paying premium)
- Asymmetric risk (downside ₹240–300, upside maybe ₹520 if bull case)
- Better opportunities elsewhere (other stocks at better entry prices)

**Profit:** +₹1,550 on ₹10K investment (~15.5% gain in 6 weeks)

**Next action:**
- **If stock falls to ₹350:** Add back ₹15–20K. Risk/reward improving.
- **If stock falls to ₹280–300:** Add back ₹20–30K aggressively. Fair value zone.
- **If stock rises to ₹550+:** Avoid entirely. Bull case priced in; downside risk too high.
- **If Africa shows positive results (Q4 FY26–Q2 FY27):** Reconsider at ₹300–350 range.

---

## Final Judgment

VBL is a quality consumer business with proven management and real distribution moat—but it's currently trading at a price that assumes best-case outcomes (18–20% growth, Africa 20%+ ROCE, margins staying intact). We believe base case (10–13% growth, 13–14% margins, Africa 15–18% ROCE) is 50% probability, not 25%. That's why fair value is ₹240–300, not ₹401.

We bought at ₹401 to test the thesis with real capital. Stock rallied to ₹463 within weeks. This was opportunity to take profits and wait for actual catalysts (Africa profitability, Sting scaling, FCF inflection) to confirm base case before re-entering. We exited, banked 15% gain, and are now waiting for re-entry at ₹280–350 when risk/reward becomes compelling again.

VBL is a buy, but at the right price, not at today's premium. Patience compounds more than capital—especially in a business already compounding at 20%+ ROCE.
