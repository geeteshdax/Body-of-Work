# VARUN BEVERAGES LIMITED 

## SUMMARY

**Who is VBL?** PepsiCo's largest bottler and distributor outside the US. Manufactures and sells Pepsi, Mountain Dew, Aquafina, Sting across India + Africa. 500K+ retail outlets, 56 bottling plants.

**What's working?** Exceptional growth (24.6% revenue CAGR over 5 years). Strong capital returns. Distribution moat is real and defensible. Management executes operationally.

**What's broken?** Campa disruption proved pricing power is eroding. Africa capex (₹1,500–2,000 Cr) is unproven investment.
