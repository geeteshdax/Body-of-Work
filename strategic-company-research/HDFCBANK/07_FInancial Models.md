# FINANCIAL MODELS

**Read Time:** 5 minutes  
**Key Takeaway:** Fair value is ₹640–700, implying 13–15% downside from current price. We anchor on P/B linked to sustainable RoE, with DCF as sanity check.  
**Who Should Read This:** Investors wanting to understand our valuation logic, founders studying how capital-intensive businesses are valued

---

## The Insight

Valuation is simple: What is a fair price for a bank earning 15% RoE?

Using the formula **Fair P/B = Sustainable RoE / Cost of Equity**, a bank earning 15% with a 9% cost of equity should trade at 1.67x book value. Book value is ₹365 per share. Fair value: ₹610.

But HDFC could earn 16–17% if its challenges resolve. That pushes fair value to ₹669–₹731.

Current price is ₹774.65. Somewhere between slightly overvalued (bull case) and materially overvalued (conservative case).

---

## Why This Matters

HDFC's valuation hinges entirely on **whether RoE recovers.** RoE recovery depends on NIM recovery (not loan growth or cost cuts). If NIM stays at 3.38%, RoE stays at 15%, and fair value is ₹610. If NIM recovers to 3.6%, RoE improves to 16%, and fair value is ₹669.

This is testable quarterly. Watch NIM. Everything else follows.

---

## Evidence & Reasoning

**Three scenarios (equal weight on probability):**

| Scenario | RoE | Cost of Equity | Fair P/B | Fair Value | Probability |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Conservative** | 15.0% | 9.0% (governance risk premium) | 1.67x | ₹610 | 35% |
| **Base Case** | 16.0% | 8.75% (modest governance haircut) | 1.83x | ₹669 | 50% |
| **Bull Case** | 17.0% | 8.5% (governance resolved) | 2.0x | ₹731 | 15% |

**What drives each scenario:**

**Conservative:** NIM stays 3.38%. CASA keeps declining. Governance issues escalate (RBI enforcement action, deposit outflows). RoE stuck at 15%. No recovery path visible.

**Base Case:** NIM recovers to 3.55–3.6% by FY27–28 (deposit repricing working). CASA stabilizes at 35%. New permanent chair restores confidence by Q2 FY27. RoE improves to 16% as operating leverage flows. This is most likely.

**Bull Case:** All headwinds resolve faster. NIM recovers to 3.7%. CASA bounces to 37%. Loan growth accelerates to 14–16%. RoE reaches 17%. Governance issue fully contained. Less than 20% probability this happens cleanly.

**Cross-check (P/E method):**

Forward normalized EPS (FY28) ranges from ₹53–₹60 depending on scenario. Fair P/E multiples (using PEG 2.2) range from 13–22x. This converges roughly with P/B valuations when adjusted for growth.

---

## The Implication

**Probability-weighted fair value: ₹659** (range ₹640–700)

Current price of ₹774.65 is **13–15% overvalued** on base case assumptions. Even in bull case, stock is only 5% overvalued.

For a 30% margin of safety, buy at ₹459–₹490. Not close to current price.
