Why I Exited at ₹1,447 (The First Mistake)

I exited because I had no thesis.

At ₹1,436, I owned HCL because:

It's a large-cap, quality company
Management has a track record
Dividend yield ~4%
AI tailwinds exist

But I couldn't answer: What is the bull case? What is the bear case? At what price is it expensive? At what price is it cheap? What would I sell it for?

Without answers to these questions, I had no business owning it. So when it barely moved, I took the trade off the books.

The lesson: Position without thesis = emotional trade, not investment. I learned this the hard way on HCL.

Why I Re-Entered at ₹1,225 (The Correct Thesis)

I re-entered because now I have a thesis.

At ₹1,225, I'm paying a 10% premium to probability-weighted fair value. That's acceptable if I believe the Bull Case has >20% probability (I do) and I'm willing to hold 3–5 years (I am).
