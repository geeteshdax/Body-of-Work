# Strategic Company Research

Independent research on individual businesses.
Each study starts with one question: where does the market's 
reading of this business differ from what its structure 
actually supports?

---

## What a Company Research Document Is

A structured examination of how a business competes, 
where its advantages hold under pressure, and what the 
numbers reveal when read against the business model — 
not in isolation from it.

A serious attempt to understand a business completely.

## What This Demonstrates

Most company analysis starts with the financials.
This work starts with the business.

Financials confirm or challenge what the business model 
already implies. Reading them in isolation produces 
numbers without context. Reading them against the 
structure produces a view.

That sequence matters.
